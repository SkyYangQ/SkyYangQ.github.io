# 🔍 About Me

I am Qihang Yang, an M.Sc. student in Computer Science at the University of Hong Kong. Previously, I was a Research Assistant at the Institute of Information Engineering, Chinese Academy of Sciences, where I worked on reliable code generation and LLM post-training. At UESTC, I conducted research on uncertainty-aware multimodal perception for autonomous driving.
My research interests include **trustworthy AI, multimodal learning, computer vision, uncertainty estimation, and large language models**. I am particularly interested in building trustworthy learning systems through principled modeling and rigorous empirical evaluation.
